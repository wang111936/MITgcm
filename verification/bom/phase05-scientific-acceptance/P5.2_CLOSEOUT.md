## 4. Defects found and resolved during admission

Earlier probes are diagnostic evidence and are not accepted P5.2 results:

1. The final OFFLINE request needs a following record for time interpolation.
   The input now supplies one explicit 87,300 s read-ahead record and uses
   `offlineTimeOffset=450.` so record zero represents the exact time-zero
   endpoint.
2. OpenMPI rejects out-of-range coordinates passed to `MPI_Cart_rank` for a
   non-periodic Cartesian topology. `INI_PROCS` now assigns `MPI_PROC_NULL`
   to physical west/east/south/north neighbors before the invalid call while
   retaining the existing nested-child coordinate handling.
3. An Nx-by-Ny native C-grid record stores the west U and south V faces but
   not the positive physical east/north faces required by C-to-A colocation.
   BOM now completes only those missing faces in its own work arrays with a
   one-sided linear continuation before `ROTATE_UV2EN_RL`, in both legacy and
   transactional field paths. The correction recognizes compile-time and
   runtime non-periodicity, is exact for the frozen affine field, and never
   mutates MITgcm's `uVel` or `vVel` arrays.

A temporary inverse-collocated input could hide the third defect, but it
violated the frozen native C-grid contract and was rejected. A serial periodic
probe was also rejected because wrapped outer halos changed the frozen
non-periodic experiment. EXCH2 remains outside the frozen Phase 5 scope and
was not used by the accepted gate.

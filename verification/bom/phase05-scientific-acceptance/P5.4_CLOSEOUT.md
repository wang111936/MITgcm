## 3. Production defects found and corrected

P5.4 exposed and fixed two production defects rather than masking them in the
test fixtures:

1. Fresh biological owners did not receive the frozen initial biology and
   lineage state. `bom_read_initial.F` now initializes `S=bomS0`,
   `parent_id=0` and `birth_count=0`, derives `bomNextId` from the maximum
   admitted ID, and rejects ID overflow.
2. A no-event step with an active spring law could clear the accepted spring,
   neighbor and raft graph. `bom_event_transaction_p43.F` now clears that
   state only when `bomSpringLaw='NONE'`, preserving the accepted graph during
   ordinary event-free advances.

All affected manufactured, restart and endurance cases passed after these
corrections. No frozen scientific tolerance, test interval, rank matrix or
acceptance criterion was relaxed.

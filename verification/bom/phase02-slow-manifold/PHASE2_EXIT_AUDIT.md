split runs match, corruption fails before publication, and schema-1 LEEW
compatibility is retained. Changed-decomposition BOM restart is explicitly

filesystem layout, affinity and scaling remain Phase 5 work. They do not block

rollback contracts. It must not introduce an O(N-squared) production neighbor
path, and it must keep biology, beaching and target-site HPC work in their
assigned later phases.

## 1. Audit method

The audit derived its checklist from `P1.3_INTERFACE_FREEZE.md`, inspected the
production sources and current verification drivers, required a clean exact
head for every gate, checked the lifecycle checksum manifest, and treated
missing direct coverage as an open finding. That process found and closed one
coverage gap: P1-I01 previously combined separate zero-kernel and release
evidence; the final gate now executes ALIVE and multiple WAITING records
through the complete zero-RHS production caller.

The audit does not treat a successful process status, source search, or
component test as proof of a broader caller contract when a direct production
scenario is required.

tested only the spherical native rate. Commit `0458910a9bb484aab8901d1a17046e3804e82165`
adds direct nonzero-latitude pure-east and pure-north analytic displacement

## 4. Source and transaction audit

The final source inspection confirms:

- `BOM_RELEASE_STEP` is stateless and gives state errors precedence over
  release errors; it scales endpoint tolerance, guards age addition, and
  distinguishes future no-op, active motion, and zero-duration refresh;
- `BOM_SUBSTEP_SETUP/BOM_SUBSTEP_BOUNDS` guard division and integer
  conversion and force the last endpoint to `myTime`;
- RK2/RK4 remain stateless trial kernels and publish final-position
  diagnostics only after a successful FINAL refresh;
- `BOM_MAIN` writes the authoritative position, status, age, indices, ocean,
  wind, and drift fields only in the successful candidate branch;
- a failed slot contributes contextual time/iteration/substep, rank/tile/slot,
  ID, stage/code, position/index, wet weight, CFL, age, and release data before
  collective termination;
- `BOM_CHECK_STATE` validates counts before indexing, compact tails, positive
  IDs, ALIVE/WAITING semantics, all real fields, exact owner/stencil/index
  consistency, immutable owner count, and exact two-word global uniqueness.

The runtime time/substep preflight precedes field construction. This is a
documented safety refinement of the narrative ordering in section 6.3 of the
freeze: it prevents invalid time/range input from publishing a field snapshot,
does not touch particle state, and preserves the frozen field-label and
integration semantics. P1-N01b and all field/setup regressions pass.
